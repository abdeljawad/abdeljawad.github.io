ATHKAR – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the ATHKAR app for Android (package name com.abdeljawad.athkar), developed by Ahmed Abdeljawad as an individual developer.

Summary

ATHKAR does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app does not have permission to access the internet, and it contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or user accounts.

Information stored on your device

So that the app opens the way you left it, it keeps the following on your device only:

  • the language you chose (English or Arabic) and the display theme (light, dark or system);
  • your text-size choices and which parts to show (transliteration, translation, source);
  • whether vibration is turned on when counting;
  • which athkar collections you completed today (only the date, so the home screen can show a check mark).

This information never leaves your device through the app, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you have enabled Android's own backup to your Google account, Android may include these settings in that backup. This backup is a feature of your device and your Google account; the developer has no access to it.

Links to other websites

Some entries link to the source of a supplication on external websites (for example hisnmuslim.com or turath.io). These links open in your web browser only when you tap them. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Permissions

The only permission the app uses is vibration, so the counter can vibrate when you count; you can turn this off in the app's settings. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal information.

Children

ATHKAR is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Ahmed Abdeljawad at abdeljawad.ahmed@gmail.com.
